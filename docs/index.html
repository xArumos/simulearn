Talk to the SimuLearn child model

You play the teacher or caregiver. Two AI models answer each line you type: an ordinary AI, and our model trained on real conversations between children and adults. Tell us which one sounds more like a real child.

Open the demo →
Password: type any username and the team password (ask Justin Perkins).
The first visit after a quiet spell can take a minute or two while the demo wakes up. Each reply takes a few seconds.

How to test it

  1. Type your name in the Your name box, so we know who tested.
  2. Pick the child's age.
  3. Type what an adult would say to a child, then press Enter. Short, casual lines work best.
  4. Compare the two answers. Click 👍 or 👎 on any reply. You can add a note first if you like.
  5. Click New conversation to start a different scenario.

Please don't type real names or personal details about real children. Conversations and ratings are saved for the research team.

Lines to try

Playing with blocks

  1. what are you making over there?
  2. ooh. is that a house or a garage?
  3. uh oh, it fell down. what happened?

Snack time

  1. okay, sit down please. it's time to eat.
  2. do you want milk or juice?
  3. what did you have for lunch at school today?

Reading a picture book

  1. look at that dog. what's he doing?
  2. why do you think he's sad?
  3. what do you think happens next?
